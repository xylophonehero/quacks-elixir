**B1 (S2): "Decisions, Decisions..." (P11) in rounds 1–2 shows only "Droplet 2 forward".**
Steps: base game, the card comes up in round 2. Expected: two buttons, "Droplet 2 forward" and "Take a purple 1" (the card text says "OR take a purple token"). Actual: one button. Cause: `Fortune.takes/2` filters on `Game.available?/2`, which applies the shop rule "purple from round 3". The rulebook limits only *buying*. `docs/research/rulebook.md` says "purple only if book is out", which is ambiguous, so Nick must make a ruling. If the rule stays as it is, the dialog must say why purple is missing. Screenshot: `decisions-no-purple-m.png`.

**B2 (S3): A reload reopens the current round's fortune card.**
Steps: draw 2 chips in round 1 and reload (desktop). Or reload during the shop phase (mobile). Expected: the state comes back with no dialog, because the card was already acknowledged. Actual: "Round N: a new card" opens again. The pot, white count and log restore correctly. Screenshots: `edge-reload-midround-d.png`, `reload-shop-card-reopens-m.png`.

**B3 (S3): The round-results replay runs again after a reload.**
Steps: end a round, close the results, reload during the shop phase. Expected: no replay (the handoff criterion "replay does not replay"). Actual: the results sheet opens again with the Skip link and the replay runs again. `replay-done` is only a client class (`on_close={JS.add_class("replay-done")}`) and is not kept anywhere. A plain socket disconnect + reconnect does NOT reopen the sheet; only a full reload does. Screenshot: `reload-shop-results-m.png`.

**B4 (S3): The rename is dropped when you tap a colour inside the debounce window.**
Steps: on the configure screen, type a new name, then tap a colour swatch at once (in my script: blur, then the colour click less than 300 ms later). Expected: the new name and the new colour are both kept. Actual: the colour changes, the name goes back to the old value, and the input shows the old value. Screenshot: `rename-lost-after-colour.png`.

**B5 (S3): The host leaves the lobby and the remaining player is half-locked.**
Steps: host Alice + Bob + bot in the lobby. Alice goes to `/`. Expected: Bob becomes host with full controls, or he gets an explanation. Actual: Bob sees an enabled "Start game". The Players −/+, all expansion and option controls stay disabled, the empty seat has no "+ Add bot", Bob has no "host" badge, and the "X sets the game up" line goes away. "Start game" then silently starts a 2-player game. This matches the `starter?/2` vs `host?/2` design, but there is no text that tells the player. Screenshot: `host-left-lobby-d.png`.

**B6 (S3): Safety Procedure lets you place a white chip that explodes the pot.**
Round 8: the choice buttons are chip images labelled only "3", "1", ... The bot-driven seat picked white 3 at white 5 and exploded (white 8). Placing it is probably legal, but nothing warns you. Add a warning, or say "explodes" on that button. Screenshots: `base-r8-fortune-after-brew-m.png`, `safety-procedure-m.png`.

**B7 (S3): On desktop, "Spend rubies" opens on top of the open Round results sheet.**
The results sheet (with its own OK) stays open behind it and the two dialogs overlap. After "Done" the results sheet is still open. Screenshot: `base-r2-rubies-d.png`.

**B8 (S3, environment): In a background tab the shop sheet stops halfway.**
When the tab was hidden (`document.visibilityState == "hidden"`), the shop opened half-way and stayed there until the next paint. The console logged `InvalidStateError: Transition was aborted because of invalid state. Document hidden`. A real player is not likely to hit this, but a player who switches tabs during the replay could. Screenshot: `hidden-tab-shop-stalled-m.png`.
