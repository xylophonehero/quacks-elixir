**N1 (S2): the Options `<details>` closes on every patch.**
Steps: host on the lobby, open "Options", tap "Starting rubies +". Or keep Options open while another player changes colour. Expected: Options stays open. Actual: it closes at once. The host must reopen Options after each step, so a 2-step change (7 → 9) needs 4 taps, and a guest's change closes the host's section. The value does change (verified 1 → 2 → 1). Screenshots: `lobby-options-open-m.png`, `lobby-options-closed-by-remote-m.png`, `lobby-options-after-step-m.png`.

**N2 (S3): the handover applies the new host's saved settings.**
Steps: Bob (fresh profile, defaults: 2 players) creates a game, Alice joins, Bob goes to `/`. Expected: Alice becomes host and the table keeps Bob's settings. Actual: the toast is correct, but the table jumps to 3 players with Herb Witches, Alchemists, reverse pot and locoweed III on. These are Alice's saved settings from her last game. When Bob comes back he is host again and the changed settings stay. Screenshots: `host-handover-m.png`, `host-returns-d.png`.

**N3 (S3): the log narrates one resume twice.**
"Alice: Stopped (may resume while others brew)", then "Alice: Resume brewing" and "Alice: Resumed brewing". The log also mixes imperative and past tense: "Use flask" + "Returned white 1 to the bag", "Bob: Buy nothing", "Bought orange 1". Screenshot: `edge-hidden-tab-return-d.png` (Log panel).

**N4 (S3): an empty rubies step.**
With 1 ruby and nothing to buy, the "Spend rubies" sheet opens with only "You have 1 ruby." and "Done" (expansion game, round 2, Alice). The step costs a tap and changes nothing. Skip it, or show it only when a spend is possible. No screenshot (it was overwritten; it is easy to reproduce).

**N5 (S3): status text out of step with the phase.**
In the Droplet phase (reverse pot, odd rounds) the header says "Droplet", but the status line says "Everyone brews at the same time." and the seats show "brewing" (`g2-r1-m-Droplet-Round-N-a-new-card-Drop-It.png`). After an explosion → VP, the status line says "Everyone shops at the same time." to a player who cannot shop (`replay-open-m.png`).

**N6 (S3): the seat is lost with the cookie.**
Another agent's server on `localhost:4012` used the same Chrome profile and replaced the `_quacks_key` cookie (cookies are per host, not per port). After a reload Alice saw "All seats are taken. You are watching." and could not get her seat back (no rejoin by name). This is mostly a dev-environment hazard; in production it happens when a player clears cookies or changes browser. As a spectator, the reload also opened "Round 1 results" and the "Round 1: a new card" card on top of each other. Work-around I used: mobile on `127.0.0.1:4011`, desktop on `localhost:4011`. No screenshot (overwritten).
